Warranty Information

New clubs carry the manufacturer's warranty. Used clubs carry ours. Repairs carry ours.

New clubs

Full manufacturer warranty (typically 1–2 years against defects). We'll handle the claim for you — bring the club in and we deal with the brand.

Used clubs

Our 30-day return policy covers anything we missed. Beyond that, if a used club fails from a defect (cracked face, head separation) within 6 months of purchase, we'll repair it at no charge or credit you the purchase price toward a replacement. Normal wear, sky marks and grip wear aren't defects.

Repairs & builds

Our workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months: if a shaft we installed comes loose, a grip we installed slips, or a ferrule we set moves, we fix it free. Parts carry their own manufacturer warranties.

Not covered

Abuse (the tree lost too), water damage, alterations by someone else, and anything with a broken serial or tamper mark.
