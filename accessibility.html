Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site and the store.

Website

We build to WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard navigation, skip links, alt text on real photos, sufficient color contrast, no information conveyed by color alone (every condition grade has a number and a word). If something doesn't work with your screen reader or keyboard, email us and we'll fix it.

Store

Ground-level entrance through the front door and the roll-up door, no steps. Accessible parking out front. Accessible restroom. The simulator bay and putting green are at floor level. Ask and we'll bring clubs to you rather than the other way round.
