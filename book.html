Good to know

Before you come in

For fittings
Bring your current clubs — all of them for a full bag. Wear golf shoes and whatever you play in. Eat something; you'll hit a lot of balls.
For simulator time
Up to 4 people per bay. Our clubs are free to use. Kids are welcome with an adult. Sessions start on the hour.
For repair consults
Free, 20 minutes. Bring the clubs. We'll tell you what's wrong, what it costs, and whether it's worth it.
Lessons
With a local PGA pro on select days. 45 minutes in the bay with full data. Fitting + lesson bundles available — ask.
