Privacy Policy

We collect what we need to sell you clubs and ship them, and we don't sell it to anyone.

What we collect

Name, email, phone, shipping address, order history, and — if you opt in — your fitting specs and saved clubs so we can do a better job next time. Payment card details go directly to our payment processor; we never see or store them.

What we do with it

Fulfil orders, text you when pickup is ready, remind you about bookings, and (only if you signed up) send you new-arrival and PWR Finds emails or texts. Unsubscribe anytime with one click or by replying STOP.

What we don't do

Sell, rent or share your information with anyone except the services required to run the store (payment processing, shipping carriers, email/SMS delivery).

Cookies & analytics

Basic site analytics and the cookies needed to keep your cart. No ad-tracking pixels.

Your data

Email us and we'll show you what we have or delete it. Florida, US law applies.
