How grading works

Same scale we sell on. Same scale we buy on.

9.5Mint
Hit a handful of times at most. No marks worth mentioning. Original grip like new.
9Excellent
Light use. Faint bag chatter only under close inspection. Grooves sharp.
8Very good
Normal play wear. Sole scuffs, maybe a faint crown mark. No dents, no rattles.
7Good
Honest wear. Visible sole and face scratches, a sky mark or two. Plays perfectly.
6Fair
Well-used. Cosmetic issues we photograph and describe. Priced accordingly.
✕No thanks
Cracked faces, rattles, bent hosels, counterfeits, clubs with sentimental value priced accordingly.

Value is set by model, year, condition, shaft and what the market is actually paying — not by a chart from 2019. Aftermarket shafts and left-handed clubs usually get a bump. We'll show you the number and how we got there.

Trade-in FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk in with the clubs. Takes about 10 minutes for a single club, 20–30 for a full bag.
Cash or credit — how does that work?
You choose at the counter. Cash is cash. Store credit is 20% higher and never expires. It works on new, used, repairs, fittings, sim time — anything.
Can I trade toward a specific used club?
Yes. Save the club online, mention the SKU in your quote, and we'll hold it for 48 hours once your quote is confirmed.
Do you take old / cheap / off-brand clubs?
Mostly no for individual off-brand clubs, but we'll take them as part of a full bag and price the bag fairly.
Mail-in?
Coming. For now, if you're outside Northeast Florida, email photos and we'll quote — then we send a prepaid label for clubs over $150 in value.
What about clubs with aftermarket shafts?
Bring them. Ventus, Tour AD, Tensei, Modus, KBS — real aftermarket shafts add real value and we price them separately.
