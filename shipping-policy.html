Shipping Policy

Short version: same-day before 2pm ET on business days, insured, free over $150.

Rates

Free on orders over $150. Otherwise $12.99 for single clubs, grips, balls and accessories; $24.99 for iron sets, complete sets and bags. Local pickup in St. Augustine is always free.

Timing

Orders placed before 2:00 pm ET Monday–Friday ship the same day via UPS or USPS. Later orders and weekend orders ship the next business day. Most of the Southeast is 1–2 days; the rest of the US is 3–5.

Custom builds

Ship when the build is done, typically 3–5 business days after parts are in hand. We'll email you when it's on the bench and again when it's boxed.

Packaging

Every club ships in a club box with the head wrapped and the shaft sleeved. Sets are individually wrapped. If a carrier damages something, we replace it — file nothing, just email us a photo.

International

Not yet. Sign up for the newsletter and we'll tell you when Canada goes live.
