What happens

You hit balls. We shut up and watch.

First ten minutes are your gamer. We need a baseline before we change anything. Then we start moving one variable at a time — shaft, loft, head — and we show you the numbers on every swing. You'll know why a club won, not just that it did.

You leave with a spec sheet you own. Buy from us, buy elsewhere, keep what you have — the sheet is yours.
