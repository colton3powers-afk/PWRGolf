Why PWR?

A golf warehouse that knows its stuff.

01

We actually work on clubs

Loft & lie machine, shaft puller, swing-weight scale, grip station. The workbench is in the middle of the store because it's the middle of the business.

02

Honest grading

Ten-point scale, exact-club photos, a note about that sky mark. If we wouldn't buy it at that price, we don't list it at that price.

03

Trade-ins pay more in credit

Cash if you want it. Store credit if you're smart — it's 20% higher and it goes toward anything on the floor.

04

Fit first, sell second

Launch monitor, every major brand's fitting cart, and a fitting fee that comes straight off the clubs you buy.
