About PWR

No mission statement. We buy, sell, build and fit golf clubs, and we're obsessive about it. That's the whole story.

The short version

We got tired of golf stores that don't know golf.

PWR started as a garage, a loft-and-lie machine bought off a retiring club builder, and a habit of buying too many used irons. It turned into a warehouse in St. Augustine with a workbench in the middle of the floor, a putting green, a sim bay, and racks of new and used clubs that turn over every week.

We're family-owned. We play. We work on our own stuff at the same bench we work on yours. If you want a store where the person selling you a driver has actually pulled a shaft, this is it.

What we actually do

Buy. Sell. Trade. Fit.

Buy — new clubs from every major brand, and pre-owned clubs we've inspected, graded and photographed one at a time.
Sell — bring us your clubs. Cash or credit, same day.
Trade — old driver in, better driver out, difference paid in credit.
Fit — launch monitor, every brand, fee credited to whatever you buy.

About the cat.

Florida's only big cat — quiet, patient, and it does not miss. It's been on our shirts since before we had a storefront. You'll see it stitched on the towels, stamped on the ferrules of our custom builds, and hiding at the bottom of this website. That's as corporate as the branding gets.
